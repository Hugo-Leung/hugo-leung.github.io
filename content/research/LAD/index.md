LAD GEM Detector System
The Large Acceptance Detector (LAD) in Hall C is designed to operate in a high‑rate environment while providing precise charged‑particle tracking for nucleon structure measurements. I contribute to LAD through commissioning, characterization, and analysis of its GEM detector system.
Role and Contributions

Lead the setup, calibration, and optimization of GEM tracking modules for LAD operations.
Develop and refine analysis procedures for high‑rate performance, including efficiency studies, alignment, and cluster‑finding.
Work on improving reconstruction quality under challenging backgrounds, ensuring LAD’s readiness for future measurements.
Support integration of test‑beam results and detector performance studies into Hall C experiment planning.
